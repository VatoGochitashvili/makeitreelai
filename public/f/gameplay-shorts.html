Gameplay ShortsLive

Your clip over Minecraft or Fortnite footage — or an AI voice reading it, source never shown.

Two formats. Split screen keeps the real clip and its audio on top with gameplay running underneath. Brainrot drops the source footage entirely: gameplay fills the frame and an AI voice reads the rewritten script.

The footage is not ours to ship — it belongs to whoever recorded it — so it is a library you fill. Upload your own from the Studio, or drop files in assets/backgrounds/.

Both rendered by the pipeline from the same source. The parkour course behind them is generated, not captured — we can only publish footage we own, so we drew our own. Point it at real Minecraft or Fortnite you recorded and nothing else changes.

How it works

  1. Pick a background from your library
  2. Split: stack the clip over the gameplay, keep the original audio
  3. Brainrot: rewrite, speak, and let the gameplay fill the frame
  4. Start at a random point in the footage so clips don't all look alike
