AutopilotLive

Connect your podcast feed once. New episodes clip and queue themselves.

Every clip tool stops at “here are your clips”. The actual job is being on TikTok five times a week, forever — and that part is still manual everywhere else.

Connect an RSS feed once. The server checks it every half hour, and a new episode is clipped and spaced one post a day at the hour you choose. Nobody has to open the site.

How it works

  1. Connect your show's RSS feed once
  2. The first sync marks the back catalogue as seen, so nothing old is clipped
  3. Each pass takes the newest unseen episode only
  4. Finished clips are spaced one a day at your chosen hour
