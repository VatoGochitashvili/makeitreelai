Voice OverLive

Six AI voices that read a hook, or narrate the whole thing.

A synthetic voice can carry a clip on its own, which is the entire premise of the format that took over TikTok.

GPT rewrites the moment as something meant to be read aloud, TTS speaks it, and then the spoken audio goes back through Whisper. That last step looks circular but it is the only way to caption narration: we know the words, not when each one lands.

How it works

  1. GPT rewrites the moment for the ear, at about 2.6 words a second
  2. TTS speaks it in the chosen voice
  3. Whisper transcribes the speech back to get word timings
  4. Captions are burned from those timings
