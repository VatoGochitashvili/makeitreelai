Smart FrameLive

Keeps the speaker in frame at a sane zoom, not shoved in your face.

A 9:16 crop of a 16:9 video keeps about a third of the width and magnifies 1.78×. That is geometry, not a setting — which is why clips from most tools feel like they are pressed against the speaker's face.

The only way out is to stop filling the frame edge to edge. Balanced keeps roughly half the width, centred on wherever the speaker actually is, sized to fill about two thirds of the height, with the rest blur-filled. Same shot, a third less zoom.

How it works

  1. Sample the section at low resolution and measure per-column motion
  2. Take the busiest column as the speaker, unless the motion is spread out
  3. Place the crop window there and clamp it inside the frame
  4. Blur-fill above and below instead of cropping harder
