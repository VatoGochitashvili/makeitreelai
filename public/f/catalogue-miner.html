Catalogue MinerLive

Reads your whole back catalogue and finds the best moments across every episode.

Every other clip tool starts from the file you upload today. A podcaster with eighty episodes therefore has sixty hours of their best thinking in an archive nothing can reach — and a new customer gets nothing at all until they record again.

This reads the whole history instead. Each past episode is transcribed once, cheaply, from audio alone. The model then names the subjects the show keeps returning to, and finds the strongest moments on one of them across every episode — six answers from six episodes, not six from whichever file you happened to upload.
