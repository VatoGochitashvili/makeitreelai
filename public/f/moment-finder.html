Moment FinderLive

Finds the clip-worthy moments in any long video and ranks them.

A long video is mostly connective tissue. The job is to find the few stretches that stand on their own, and to cut them where the thought finishes rather than where a timer runs out.

Whisper transcribes with word-level timing. GPT reads the timestamped transcript and picks complete thoughts — setup and payoff, no dangling pronouns. Then the picks get snapped to sentence boundaries using the punctuation, and anything overlapping by more than half is dropped.

How it works

  1. Whisper returns segments and word timings
  2. GPT picks complete thoughts from the timestamped transcript
  3. Picks snap to sentence ends, overlaps are dropped
  4. ffmpeg cuts each moment
