Privacy

Last updated 30 August 2026

What we collect, why, and who else sees it. Written to be read rather than to be defensible.

What we collectPaymentsWho else your content reachesHow long we keep thingsYour rightsSecurityContact

What we collect

Your name, email address and a hashed password. Passwords are hashed with scrypt and a per-account salt — we cannot read yours, and neither can anyone who steals the database.

The links you paste and the files you upload, so we can make clips from them.

Transcripts of what you process. These are kept because features like the back catalogue miner search across everything you have processed; delete a video from your library and its transcript goes with it.

The clips we make for you, and basic usage counts (credits spent, videos processed) so we can bill correctly and show you what is left.

If you connect a podcast feed, its URL and the episodes we have already seen.

Payments

We do not see your card. Payment is handled by Stripe on their own pages; we receive only a customer reference and whether your subscription is active.

We never store card numbers, expiry dates or security codes, because they never reach our servers.

Who else your content reaches

OpenAI — audio is sent to Whisper for transcription and transcripts to GPT to choose moments and write titles. If you use the AI voice, your script is sent to their text-to-speech. OpenAI's API terms state that API content is not used to train their models.

Stripe — for payments, as above.

The source you point us at — when you paste a link we fetch it, so that host sees a download request.

That is the complete list. We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising, and there are no analytics or tracking scripts on this site.

How long we keep things

Generated clips are deleted after 30 days. This is a storage limit, not a policy preference — download anything you want to keep.

Uploaded source files are deleted within hours of processing.

Delete a clip from your library and the file is removed from our servers immediately, not flagged as hidden.

Close your account and everything associated with it is deleted, including transcripts and any connected feed.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of everything we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing the address below. We will do it within 30 days.

If you are in the UK or EU, the legal basis for processing your content is performance of a contract — we cannot make you clips without it.

Security

Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies. Passwords are scrypt-hashed. Card data never touches our systems.

We are a small operation and will not pretend otherwise: if we ever have a breach that affects you, we will tell you what happened and when, without waiting to have a comfortable explanation ready.

Contact

Questions about any of this: hello@makeitreel.ai.
