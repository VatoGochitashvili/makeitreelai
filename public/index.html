FAQ

Questions, answered

What kind of videos work best?

Talking-head content — podcasts, interviews, coaching, and video essays. Anything with clear speech gives the AI great material to find standout moments.

Can I clip any YouTube video?

Only clip videos you own or have the rights to. We add an ownership check before launch — please respect creators' rights.

How long does a video take?

Most videos finish in a couple of minutes. Longer podcasts take a bit more while we transcribe the full episode in chunks.

Do you post to socials for me?

The auto-post scheduler to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube is on the Creator and Pro plans and rolling out soon.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are monthly and you can cancel from your account at any time — no contracts.
